Simple by design

Three steps from surplus to smiles

No cold calls, no spreadsheets. FoodBridge handles matching, routing and proof — you just show up for the food.

01

Post surplus food

Donors list quantity, freshness and a pickup window in seconds. Add a photo and pin the exact pickup location on the map.

02

Volunteers pick up

Nearby volunteers are notified instantly, claim the listing and get turn-by-turn routing to the donor and drop-off point.

03

Track every rescue

Recipients confirm delivery end-to-end, and donors receive a CSR-ready donation certificate — full transparency, every time.
